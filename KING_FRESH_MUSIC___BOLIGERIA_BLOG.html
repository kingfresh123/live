Elmina Bakatue Fishing Festival 2026

Bakatue is celebrated by the chiefs and people of Elmina in the Central Region of Ghana. The word Bakatue translates to "the opening of the lagoon". The festival invokes divine blessings from ancestors for a rich fishing season and protection for fishermen. Activities include royal boat regattas, traditional rituals, and a grand music festival hosted by King Fresh Music.

FESTIVAL GUIDE

Bakatue Fishing Festival 2026

The Bakatue Fishing Festival takes place every year in July in the historic town of Elmina, Ghana. Highlights include traditional ceremonies to open the Benya Lagoon for fishing, colorful royal canoe regattas, and the King Fresh Music Live Stage featuring Ghana's top artists.

Venue: Benya Lagoon & Elmina Castle Grounds

Organizers: Elmina Traditional Council & Boligeria Media
